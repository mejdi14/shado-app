How feedback works

These are practice estimates, not validated pronunciation grades. Word accuracy compares speech recognition with the phrase text; it does not assess individual speech sounds. Rhythm uses duration and loudness patterns. Intonation compares relative pitch contours; music and noisy recordings can affect it. Audio extraction keeps background sounds—it does not isolate the speaker.
