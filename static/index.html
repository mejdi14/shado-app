    How feedback works

    These are practice estimates, not validated pronunciation grades. Word accuracy compares speech recognition with the phrase text; it does not assess individual speech sounds. Rhythm uses duration and loudness patterns. Intonation compares relative pitch contours; music and noisy recordings can affect it. Audio extraction keeps background sounds—it does not isolate the speaker. The line under the phrase follows the voice going up and down, relative to each speaker’s own pitch. Pauses are quiet gaps of 0.15 seconds or more. Bold words are simply the loudest ones, which are often but not always the stressed words. A saved take is stretched to the speaker’s length so the lines line up.
